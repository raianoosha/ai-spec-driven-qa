# Cart and checkout — deliberately ambiguous product requirement

A signed-in shopper can add products to a cart, remove products, and check out.

The cart should always be accurate. Checkout should collect customer information and end with a clear confirmation.

## Intentionally unresolved

- Does “accurate” include badge count, names, price, quantity, or all of them?
- Can the same product be added twice?
- Which customer fields are required and what validation applies?
- Can an empty cart be checked out?
- Is tax calculation or final total in scope?
- What exact confirmation proves completion?
